# 05 · Asset Decisions

## Real photograph of Alex — highest impact

Single highest-impact asset on the site. Goes on `/about`.

- **No stock photography of a person, under any circumstances.**
- **No AI-generated portrait.**
- Request through Arham. Until it lands: `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION: real photo of Alex]` placeholder — do not ship without.

## Hero imagery — human connection, not abstract

Direction from Alex (17 Sep call, transcript ~43:30): the current live-site hero is an AI-generated "staircase to nowhere" — abstract and disposable. He wants **something with human connection.** Not literally a stock photo of a person (that is still banned by compliance rules), but imagery that reads as *someone lives here* rather than as decorative geometry.

Options to raise for approval:
- A real photograph of Alex (same asset as `/about`, framed for hero use)
- A photograph of a real object with meaning (e.g. a desk, a book, a place in Scottsdale)
- Restrained editorial layout with no hero image at all — headline + generous whitespace
- **Not:** gradient meshes, blob shapes, abstract 3D, AI-generated environments

## Illustration: Tzur Magen 21 → 7 narrowing

Genuinely visual concept. Suggested: simple, quiet diagram (SVG). Nothing chart-like or performance-adjacent.

- Must not resemble a returns chart, growth chart, or backtest visualisation.
- Style: aligned with the "research publication" register — not "SaaS explainer."

Client's own note: "a little video for everything… doesn't have to be my face."

## Downloads — the real purpose of `/insights`

He is rebuilding a research white paper specifically so the site has something worth downloading:

> "They have some cool downloads, which I don't have yet, but I'll have to create. There's the white paper research project thing that I need to figure out how to recreate."

- Build `/insights` so a gated or ungated PDF drops in cleanly later.
- **Ask before building any email-capture gate.**

## Typography

- Serif for editorial weight (research-publication register) OR quiet geometric sans — pick one direction, don't blend.
- Body ≥ 18px, generous line-height, real contrast for 60-year-old eyes.
- No thin display weights on light backgrounds.
- Max 2 families.

## Colour

- Muted, calm. No high-saturation accents.
- Avoid the default advisor-blue trope if possible — differentiation via restraint.
- Contrast must pass WCAG AA (verify, don't eyeball).

## Icons

- Optional. If used, single line-weight family, no decorative flourishes.
- Never for "trust" or "security" badges — those read as regulated social proof.

## Do not create

- Stock people photos
- Team-page filler
- "As seen in" logo strips
- Award badges
- Result-suggesting graphs, charts, or hero visuals
