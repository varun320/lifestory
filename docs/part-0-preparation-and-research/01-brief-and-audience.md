# 01 · Brief & Audience

## The 60-second version

Alex Hilbert runs **Life Story Wealth Planning**, a small licensed RIA in Arizona. Current site is a single page pretending to be a site — nav items are anchor links scrolling the same page. No About, contact, or service pages. Almost nobody finds him via search; clients come from referrals.

The site is not a marketing funnel. It is the thing a referred stranger checks before making contact. Today when they check, his own site does not appear on page one of a name search — LinkedIn, a regulator DB, and two data brokers do.

## Who the client is

- **Alex Hilbert** — Registered Investment Advisor. Fiduciary (legally required to put clients' interests first). This shapes every decision.
- **Life Story Wealth Planning** — this project. Phoenix/Scottsdale area. Manages money for individuals. Deliberately small.
- **DriveChain Capital Management** — separate commercial lending brokerage. Different brand, different site. Do not mix.
- **Hilbert Financial Group** — closed. Must not appear anywhere on the new site.

## Who the site is for

Retired / semi-retired executives and business owners, ~60. Already wealthy, trying not to lose it. Financially literate, careful readers, marketed at their entire adult lives. Usually referred by someone they trust, then check him out quietly before making contact.

### What that means for writing & design

- Calm beats energetic. Every time.
- Specific beats impressive.
- No urgency tactics, countdowns, "limited spots", pop-ups.
- Long, plain sentences are fine — this reader reads.
- Generous type size, real contrast — sixty-year-old eyes.
- Anything that feels like pressure reads as desperation. Desperation in someone handling your retirement is disqualifying.

## The one job

Within five seconds of arrival: **this is the right person, this is a real firm, here is how to reach him.**

Search volume in his area is tiny and dominated by national firms. There is also an unrelated "Life Story Financial" that ranks well for his firm name — which is why his personal name and Arizona location must be unmistakable on every page.

### Practical consequence

**The About page is the most important page — more important than the homepage.** Give it the most care, most detail, and his real photo. The homepage exists to route people to it.
