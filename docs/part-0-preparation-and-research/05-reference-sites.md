# 05 · Reference Sites

Three lists, three reasons. Study, do not clone. Take structure, pacing, and how they handle regulated claims — never their copy, images or layouts wholesale.

## A · The client's own picks (highest weight — copy references, not design)

He was explicit: **no design references** — "I don't know of any very good designs." Design direction is ours to propose; copy direction is his.

| Site | Use for | What he said |
|---|---|---|
| **Redwood Investment Management** | COPY / NOT DESIGN | Primary reference. Philosophy "in the same ballpark of what I do" — risk-first. Likes: "illustrations are simple and clear", "a little video for everything" (wants short explainers, "doesn't have to be my face"), their downloads/lead-gen. **Dislikes strongly:** scroll storyboard — "way too much… over the top… you have to scroll really hard to get to the next section." **Do not build a scroll-jacking site.** Also flagged their "Upcoming Events" showing 2024 entries as "two and a half years out of date" — he notices staleness. **Do not build a page that will rot.** |
| **Flexible Plan Investments** (Jerry Wagner) | COPY / NOT DESIGN | "Content-wise, going to be pretty strong… ton of content." Design: "I don't like the way it is now" — better in the past. Mine for how active-management philosophy gets explained at length. |
| **Dynamic Performance Publishing** | COPY | Publisher behind an active-management piece he pulled up. Topics: active vs passive, why active management, retirement, investment theory. Intellectual territory the Insights section should occupy. |

**Outstanding:** he was asked to send URLs and has not. Find them yourself by name and confirm the right firms — similarly named companies exist in this industry (a live problem for his own firm too).

## B · Craft references — how good advisory sites are written

| Site | Why |
|---|---|
| **Ritholtz Wealth** (`ritholtzwealth.com`) | Closest model to where Alex is heading. Built the firm on content + distinct voice. "We Focus On What Works." "No hype. No shortcuts." That is the register. Confident without ever claiming results — learn the trick. |
| **Abundo Wealth** (`abundowealth.com`) | Unusually plain about what they do and how they charge. Best study for the Fees page. |
| **Facet** (`facet.com`) | Bigger and mass-market — don't copy tone. But clearest example of turning an abstract service into something a stranger understands in one screen. |
| **Kitces** (`kitces.com`) | Industry's own reference site. If unsure whether something is normal or risky in adviser marketing, the answer is usually here. |

## C · Competitive set — who actually appears in his area

| Site | Why |
