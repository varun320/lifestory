# 04 · Voice

Client's own words on how the firm should sound:

> "Plain-spoken. First person. No jargon. No exclamation marks. Calm. It is fiduciary — a professional tone, but a warm professional tone."
> — Alex Hilbert

## Rules

- **First person singular ("I")** wherever truthful. Current site says "we" throughout but it is essentially him. Check each instance — some statements are genuinely about the firm.
- **No exclamation marks anywhere.** Literal instruction.
- **Long plain sentences are fine.** This reader reads.
- **Describe process, never results.**
- **Numbers must be right.** If uncertain, leave it out and flag.

## Write like this

- "I manage portfolios for people who have already built their wealth."
- "Here is what happens when we work together."
- "I do not take custody of your money. It is held at a separate institution."
- "Most of my clients come to me because someone they trust suggested it."

## Not like this

- "Unlock your financial future!"
- "We leverage a proprietary multi-factor framework to optimise alpha."
- "Trusted by hundreds of families across Arizona."
- "Breaking through the Efficient Frontier." (current headline — replace)
