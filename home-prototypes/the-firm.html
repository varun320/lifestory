ⅠWhy this firm exists

Most portfolios sit still. Markets do not.

When markets fall, the standard answer clients hear from their adviser is to do nothing.

I do not think that is management. It is the absence of it. I built this firm to work the other way — to actually manage portfolios, reviewed and reconstituted every quarter, moved between asset classes as conditions change.

The process has a name. I call it Zermagen, from the Hebrew for fortress and shield. It is built around a single idea: risk comes first. Before I decide what to buy, I decide what could go wrong and how to guard against it.

If you were referred to me, the page most people read next is About.

Markets are people first, and mathematics a distant third.

ⅢWhat it costs

Potentially nothing.

There is no management fee. I am paid only on gains above a defined threshold — and if the strategy does not work in a given quarter, I do not get paid for that quarter.

The exact wording, and what it means in plain language, is on the Fees page →

ⅣWhere your money actually is

I never hold client money.

Your account is at a third-party custodian, in your own name. I direct how it is invested. I cannot move funds out of it, to me or to anyone else.

That distinction is the single most important structural safeguard in how I have set up the firm.
