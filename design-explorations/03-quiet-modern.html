Why this firm exists

Most portfolios sit still. Markets do not.

When markets fall, the standard answer clients hear from their adviser is to do nothing.

I do not think that is management. It is the absence of it.

I built this firm to work the other way. To actually manage portfolios — reviewed and reconstituted every quarter, moved between asset classes as conditions change, with the discretion to hold cash when nothing deserves capital. The process has a name — I call it Zermagen, which is Hebrew for fortress and shield.

Markets are people first, and mathematics a distant third.

Who this is for

Most of my clients came to me because someone they trust suggested it. If that is how you arrived here, you are the person this site is written for.

I work with a small number of individuals — usually approaching retirement or already in it, usually more focused on keeping what they have built than on growing it aggressively.

What it costs

Potentially nothing.

There is no management fee. I am paid only on gains above a defined threshold — and if the strategy does not work in a given quarter, I do not get paid for that quarter.
